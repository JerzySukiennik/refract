REFRACT — audio credits
=======================

All sampled audio in this folder is from Kenney (kenney.nl), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal). Public domain, no attribution
required — kept here anyway because credit is cheap and correct.

The beam hum, rotation ticks, receptor bells, the solve chord and its sparkle
are NOT samples: they are synthesised at runtime in js/audio.js with the Web
Audio API, so they can respond continuously to game state.
